for my thumbnails, I make sure to stick to a maximum of 3 main elements on the screen (such as the person themselves, and then the main focus of the video such as a character/moment from the video itself).

this rule helps me not only keep my thumbnails clean, but easier for the viewer to quickly glance at and understand what the video is about by reducing the cognitive load on the viewer, in comparison having lots of elements on the screen can confuse and require the viewer to process more information.

this helps them to quickly understand what the video is about in comparison to a thumbnail where it overloads the viewer with information they don't need. on request i can tweak thumbnails to the creator's liking.
